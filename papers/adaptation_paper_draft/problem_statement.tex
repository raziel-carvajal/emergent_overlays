Broadcast is a fundamental operation in Mobile Ad-Hoc Networks (MANETs) that is also use as a routing mechanism~\cite{SurveyRuiz,survey_routing}.
%
There is a large variety of broadcast protocols in the literature.\raziel{write the use of decentralized algorithms du to the lack of a global entity}
%
Following the taxonomy in~\cite{SurveyRuiz}, broadcast algorithms can be categorized in two main families.
%
Protocols of the first family use a form of controlled flooding to decide \emph{when} to forward a message, this action is based on the local knowledge that nodes collect on each message reception.
%
Protocols of the second category build a backbone of nodes where its members are responsible of forwarding any incoming message, any other node out of the backbone act as pure receiver.
%
Nodes construct and maintain this virtual overlay independently of the broadcast operations.

The performance of broadcast algorithms is mainly influenced by operation conditions such as the density of the network, and the mobility rate of nodes.
%

A comparison study~\cite{DensityMobDrivenEval} shows that the best algorithm for a given situation, such as a high density and a static network, is not necessarily the most appropriate for a different situation such as a sparse and mobile network.
