\begin{abstract}
Broadcast is a fundamental operation in Mobile Ad-Hoc Networks (MANETs).
%
A large variety of broadcast protocols have been proposed to deal with network dynamics, maximize coverage and minimize the impact of collisions.
%
These protocols follow either an unstructured approach based on the control of a flooding process or a more structured approach involving the creation of a dissemination overlay.
%
The selection of the appropriate broadcast protocol for a deployed MANET is a difficult task.
%
This paper presents the ongoing work towards a novel adaptive approach to broadcast in heterogeneous MANETs.
%
This approach allows nodes to pick dynamically the most appropriate broadcast protocols for different regions of the network.
%
We present preliminary results of an interoperability mechanism to broadcast in an scenario where more than one protocol is deployed.
%
Our results show that the energy cost tends to reduce with such an approach, keeping a high coverage of nodes improving the network throughput.
\end{abstract}
