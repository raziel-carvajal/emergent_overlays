\section{Future work and Conclusion}
Selecting what is the appropriate broadcast algorithm for a given MANET deployment scenario is a daunting task.
%
The performance of a given approach depends on its deployment context.
This makes hard to find the right balance between reliability and efficiency.
%
We present preliminary results of an hybrid approach for broadcast protocols in MANETs where an overlay is deployed in dense regions of the network and controlled flooding techniques run in sparse regions.

%
As future work, we will focus in the specifications of adaptation rules for lettings nodes decide which broadcast protocol fits better in the network.
%
This decision may be collective or individual but completely decentralized.
%
We rely on the local meta data available in nodes such as: the signal strength of received packets, remaining energy or rate of received packets.
%
Mainly, nodes velocity and density are two transversal aspects to take into consideration.
%
