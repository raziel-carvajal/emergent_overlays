\begin{abstract}
%Wireless communication has becoming a constant in our daily routine 
Wireless communication has an ubiquitous influence in our daily routine, mobile phones, IoT devices and other cutting-edge gadgets communicate with each other through specific protocols like the widely known IEEE 802.11 standard. Broadcast is a fundamental operation in MANETs with the aim of disseminating messages to reach every participant in one network, while pure-flooding efficiently spread messages its lack of performance on broadcasting metrics such as nodes reachability, saved broadcast messages, collisions rate and energy consumption, it is very well known.

Many algorithms have been proposed to performed a control flooding of broadcast messages and preserve the resources of mobile nodes. Interestingly, when it comes to decide which is the algorithm that could fit one scenario, such as a network where node's vicinity is smaller than V neighbors per transmission area and messages dissemination must reach 90\% of nodes, it is difficult named one particular algorithm due to the heterogeneous broadcast metrics that authors consider in their works.
